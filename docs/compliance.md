# Engagement evidence and confidentiality

Omni by Enterprise DNA builds the operating checks around the business's approved policy. These checks do not certify legal compliance. Sources checked 30 October 2026.

| Check | Source and meaning | What runs |
|---|---|---|
| Survey notice | [NZ privacy principle 3](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/3/) concerns explaining collection to the individual. | Missing notice evidence is flagged. The operator reviews the actual notice and permitted purpose. |
| Access review | [NZ privacy principle 5](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/5/) requires reasonable safeguards. | A missing or past access review date is flagged. Public database privileges are revoked. A deployment still needs restricted operators, backups and secure storage. |
| Retention purpose | [NZ privacy principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/) limits keeping personal information beyond its lawful need. | Missing or past retention review dates are flagged. No automatic deletion or fixed statutory retention period is invented. |
| Source confidentiality | [Culture Amp participant guidance](https://support.cultureamp.com/en/articles/8384294-general-survey-participant-faqs) explains reporting group minimums. | Counts below the configured minimum, source suppression and missing counts withhold scores before storage. |
| Export scope | [Culture Amp export guidance](https://support.cultureamp.com/en/articles/8438326-survey-export-options) distinguishes aggregate and raw reports. | Only reviewed overall question aggregates are accepted. Identifier, comment and demographic headers are rejected. |

Five responses is this base's minimum policy floor, not a legal threshold or a universal Culture Amp setting. The organisation must keep any stricter original threshold. Focus below 70, a seven-day due window and fourteen-day stale check are configurable business policies, not law. A completed check-in and completion reference are internal evidence requirements.

Australia has a conditional employee records exemption. It is not a blanket exemption for all employment-related information or external service providers. Read the [OAIC employee-records guidance](https://www.oaic.gov.au/privacy/privacy-guidance-for-organisations-and-government-agencies/organisations/employee-records-exemption) and decide the actual deployment obligations with the responsible adviser. The code does not decide legal applicability.

## What these checks cannot establish

A source reference is not proof of permission. An actor string is not authentication. A database owner can alter the audit. Header checks cannot discover private information hidden inside an innocent-looking text field. The authorised operator must inspect imports and notes. Generated files are private by default and must stay restricted, including copies and backups.

This base does not store individual survey responses or build demographic intersections. It cannot guarantee anonymity in every organisation. A minimum count alone cannot establish that an aggregate is safe to share. Do not publish outputs without the organisation's confidentiality review. Score changes do not establish causation and must not be used to infer employee-level views.

Retention review flags work. A production deletion, access or correction request requires an approved process covering database records, audit entries, drafts, exports and backups. No automatic legal notices, certifications or breach reports are sent.
